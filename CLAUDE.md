# CLAUDE.md — Digital Garden (MDRAI)

## What this project is

A personal digital garden built as part of the Master in Design for Responsible AI (MDRAI) at Elisava. It is a living knowledge space, not a blog or documentation site. Content evolves organically — seeded, grown, and cross-pollinated throughout the program (2025–2026).

## Tone and voice

- Reflective and curious, not authoritative
- Personal — written in first person
- Comfortable with uncertainty and open questions
- The garden metaphor is intentional: use it, don't fight it

## Content structure

All notes live in `content/notes/`. One flat section — no sub-categories. Use tags and internal `[[wikilinks]]` to connect ideas rather than folder hierarchy.

Files are markdown. Prefer short, linkable notes over long essays. Internal links between notes are encouraged.

## What to help with

- Drafting and refining notes and ideas
- Suggesting connections between notes via wikilinks
- Writing reflections on program themes: AI ethics, responsible design, speculative futures, human-AI interaction
- Keeping the tone personal and exploratory, not authoritative

## What NOT to do

- Don't make notes sound like blog posts or essays unless explicitly asked
- Don't add polish or "publishedness" — this is a garden, not a publication
- Don't add dates as "published on" — prefer "last updated" framing
- Don't create new folders without asking

## Program context

- **Institution:** Elisava, Barcelona
- **Program:** Master in Design for Responsible AI (MDRAI)
- **Assignment:** M1 - Becoming a digital gardener
- **Peer collaboration:** Part of a collective gardening practice — content may reference or link to peers' gardens
- **Final review:** June 2026 — assessed on authenticity, consistency, and collaboration
