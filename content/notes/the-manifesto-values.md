title: The manifesto values
date: 2025-10-02

The programme is rooted in five values from the Everything Manifesto (2019): humbleness, responsibility and accountability, plurality, empathy and tolerance, solidarity.

I've been sitting with these since the kick-off week. They're interesting because they're not a checklist — they're dispositions. You can't tick "humbleness." You can only practice it repeatedly, imperfectly, and notice when you've lost it.

The one that surprises me most is **humbleness**. It's unusual to lead with it. Most design frameworks lead with ambition, with capability, with what we can build. Humbleness says: start by acknowledging the limits of what you can know. That's a methodological position, not just a moral one. It has consequences for how you research, how you consult, how you make decisions.

**Plurality** is doing quiet work too. Not diversity — plurality. It's subtly different. Diversity can be managed, counted, boxed. Plurality is a messier commitment: to genuinely holding contradictory perspectives without resolving them prematurely. That's uncomfortable. It's supposed to be.

I'm still unsure how to operationalise **solidarity** in the context of design practice. Solidarity implies action alongside affected communities — not on their behalf. But most design processes, even well-intentioned ones, still position the designer as the one who frames the problem.

These values aren't a solution. They're more like a set of questions I'm going to be asking for a long time.
